When to act

Check after the daily close. A confirmed allocation change is acted on at the following trading session's open. The first opposite verdict alone does not change the allocation.

The taxable-account exception

This check applies only when an exit is already due and you own TQQQ. Apply it to each purchase separately.

Postpone that purchase's sale only while
Gain > 0ANDDays held ≤ 365ANDQQQ drawdown > −20%

All three must be true together. If any one is false, that purchase's sale is eligible to proceed with the exit. Monthly purchases can qualify differently, leaving a temporary mix of TQQQ and TLT.

The market signal above is the shared base rule. Purchase dates, cost basis and actual holdings determine a personal deferral decision. The 365-day rule is the quoted strategy condition. Confirm long-term tax status with your broker, especially around a leap year.

What the drawdown means

The −20% check measures QQQ's decline. TQQQ targets three times the index's daily return, so an account can lose substantially more. TLT also moves in price and can fall while stocks fall.

In the linked whitepaper's historical T-bill tests, the reported worst before-tax decline was approximately 47% for the base rule and 57% with deferral. The author used reconstructed TQQQ returns before 2010. These figures describe that historical test and its assumptions.
